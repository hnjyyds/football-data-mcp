# Exact Margin Methodology

Use this reference whenever 让球胜平负, `让平`, exact-margin thinking, low-priced favorites, or parlay leg precision matters.

## Core Principle

让球胜平负 is a score-difference market, not only a strength market. Do not use it merely as a warning against 胜平负. Build the expected margin first, then map it to the Jingcai line.

Examples:

- Home `+1`: `让平` means the home team loses by exactly one goal.
- Home `-1`: `让平` means the home team wins by exactly one goal.
- Home `-2`: `让胜` means the home team wins by three or more goals; `让平` means it wins by exactly two.

The key question is not "who is stronger?" but "where does the score-difference cluster concentrate?"

## Margin Band Board

Before choosing an HHAD result, build margin bands:

| Margin Band | Football Meaning | Jingcai Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| draw / favorite fails | favorite superiority does not become a win | Is HAD too thin or draw protection required? |
| exact one-goal win | favorite better but margin capped | Does this map to `让平` on -1, or underdog +1 `让平`? |
| exact two-goal win | favorite controls but may stop after separation | Does this map to `让平` on -2 or `让胜` on -1? |
| three-plus win | favorite creates repeatedly or opponent collapses | Does this justify high handicap cover such as -2 `让胜`? |

For away favorites or underdogs, translate the same bands from the actual winner's perspective, then map back to the official home-team let number.

## When `让平` Deserves Primary Attention

Upgrade `让平` from a protection idea to a primary candidate when most of these are true:

- The stronger side has a credible win path, but multi-goal cover is not cleanly supported.
- The weaker side has resistance: low block, goalkeeper form, physical defending, counters, set pieces, or motivation to protect goal difference.
- The weaker side's full upset route is limited, so the live risk is narrow loss rather than outright win.
- The favorite's attack is good enough to score, but not a repeated chance machine; finishing, rotation, tempo, or tactical matchup caps the margin.
- Table state or schedule makes a controlled win acceptable, reducing late-game push after the first or second goal.
- Total-goals and score clusters point to 0-1, 1-2, 1-0, or 2-1 more than to blowout or stalemate.
- The `让平` price pays for precision risk better than a thin HAD result or a speculative `让胜/让负`.

For an underdog on `+1`, `让平` usually means: favorite direction is real, but underdog resistance keeps the loss to exactly one.

For a favorite on `-1` or `-2`, `让平` usually means: favorite superiority is real, but the most likely stopping point is exactly the let margin.

## Exact One-Goal Upgrade Pattern

Do not leave `让平` as only `高波动观察` when the match has a credible favorite/strong-side win path and independent underdog scoring resistance. Promote it into the main candidate board, and allow it to become `首选`, when most of these align:

- The favorite/strong side is more likely to win, but the evidence supports one-goal separation more than clean control: underdog must chase, can score, or has a transition/set-piece outlet.
- The weak side's best losing script is active, not passive: it can turn 2-0 into 2-1, 1-1 into 1-2, or force the favorite to keep attacking without guaranteeing a second-margin cushion.
- TTG and score prices keep 2-1, 3-2, 1-2, or 0-1 in the low/visible cluster, while 2+ margin prices are not clearly superior.
- HHAD `让平` pays better for the exact-margin risk than raw win, and `让胜/让负` either overstates dominance or is too broad to express the score path.
- In parlays, two `让平` legs are acceptable only as high-volatility exact-margin structure unless both legs have independent one-goal evidence; do not mark them stable just because they correlate with favorite wins.

When this pattern appears, compare `让平` head-to-head against the low-priced HAD result and the broad fail-to-cover result. If the evidence chain says "favorite wins, underdog scores/resists, one-goal band is the clearest cluster", `让平` is no longer a cold side note; it is a candidate for `首选`.

## When High Handicap Cover Is More Honest

Upgrade `让胜` on a negative home handicap, or the equivalent clear-cover outcome, only when the evidence supports repeated scoring:

- Large ability gap plus current form, not reputation alone.
- Repeated chance creation from open play, set pieces, pressing traps, or transition routes.
- Opponent weakness that worsens after conceding: poor defensive spacing, fatigue, low morale, must-chase game state, or goalkeeper/center-back issues.
- Motivation to keep attacking: goal difference, qualification tiebreak, statement win, or no reason to conserve.
- Total-goals and score clusters keep 3-0, 4-0, 3-1, or similar cover scores live.
- HHAD and totals do not contradict the blowout script.

## Final-Round Cover Reversal

Do not over-upgrade exact-margin protection merely because a favorite can accept a controlled result. In final-round group matches, test whether the cover path becomes stronger after the first goal.

Upgrade the clear-cover side, or at least keep it level with `让平`, when several of these are true:

- The opponent must chase a win or goal difference after conceding.
- The favorite's second unit or late substitutes are high quality and likely to press for minutes, records, or coach trust.
- The favorite has repeated chance creation from the previous matches, not only one low-repeat finishing spike.
- The weaker side's attack is too blunt to punish the favorite for pushing, so chasing mainly exposes transition space.
- Prior match data show the weak side fades, loses discipline, or concedes late separation goals.
- Score clusters include 2-0, 3-0, 3-1, or 4-2 as naturally as 1-0 or 2-1.

If this pattern appears, do not hide `让胜/让负` as only a high-volatility observation. Put it head-to-head against `让平` and explain which second-half game state is more likely.

## Red Flags

Downgrade HHAD precision when:

- Margin bands are spread across draw, one-goal, and two-plus outcomes.
- The favorite may stop pushing after taking the lead.
- The underdog has enough counter/set-piece threat to turn 2-0 into 2-1.
- Late lineup news, weather, pitch, schedule, or tournament incentives make tempo uncertain.
- The result relies on public story, ranking, or team fame more than chance-creation evidence.
- The candidate is chosen only because its odds are more attractive than HAD.
- The analysis assumes final-round tempo drop without checking goal-difference, top-spot, substitute, or chase-state incentives.

## Output Requirement

For every serious HHAD view, include:

- `比分差簇`: the main expected margin band and two to four score examples.
- `关键分叉`: the event that moves the match from one band to another, such as early goal, underdog equalizer, red card, or favorite tempo drop.
- `最怕比分`: the single score that kills the chosen HHAD result most naturally.
- `竞彩映射`: how the official let number maps the margin band to `让胜`, `让平`, or `让负`.

Final stance must say why the exact margin is better than raw 胜平负, total-goals, or skip.
