# Motivation Methodology

Use this reference for every group-stage, league-table, qualification, relegation, cup, or two-leg context. Do not treat "战意" as a one-line motivation note.

## Required Checks

Collect and explain:

1. Current points, ranking/standing, goal difference, goals for/against, and tie-break rules if visible.
2. Remaining opponents and schedule difficulty for both teams.
3. What a win changes: qualification, top spot, avoiding a strong opponent, goal-difference cushion, or only morale.
4. What a draw changes: acceptable holding result, bad but survivable result, or strategically useless result.
5. What a loss changes: elimination pressure, must-win final round, goal-difference damage, or manageable risk.
6. Whether goal difference gives the favorite a reason to keep attacking after leading.
7. Whether the underdog has reason to protect a draw, chase a win, or wait until late.
8. How motivation changes match state: early aggression, caution, low block, second-half push, or tempo management.
9. In final-round group matches, whether a simple "draw is enough" or "already qualified" story is contradicted by top-spot incentives, goal difference, host pressure, substitute audition, coach rotation quality, or a weaker opponent that must open the game.

## Jingcai Translation

- If both teams benefit from a draw, increase 防平 and low-tempo score clusters.
- If the favorite only needs a controlled win, downgrade 让胜 and high total-goals ranges.
- If goal difference matters strongly, keep 让胜/让负 and 3+ goals alive, but require tactical ability to create repeatedly.
- If the underdog must win, the game may open after conceding; raise late-goal and 3/4-goal scripts.
- If the underdog can survive with a narrow loss, let-ball protection may be stronger than 胜平负 upset.
- If a team leads and the table rewards conserving energy, expect tempo drop and exact-margin risk.
- Do not turn final-round caution into an automatic anti-cover rule. If the stronger side has repeatable chance creation and the opponent must chase, a 1-0 script can become 2-0 or 3-0 quickly.
- Treat "rotation" as neutral until quality is checked. Strong-team substitutes may increase tempo, pressing, and individual audition effort; weak-team absences may reduce resistance more than rotation reduces favorite quality.
- When one side only needs a draw but the other must win, build two second-half scripts: a low-risk stalemate script and a chase-state script after the first goal. Compare which one maps better to HHAD and totals.

## Output Requirement

Include a `战意和小组形势` section:

- `当前局面`: points, ranking, goal difference, remaining opponents.
- `赢/平/输后果`: separate for both teams.
- `比赛行为推导`: who can accept a draw, who must push, who may slow down after leading.
- `末轮反向检查`: why the match might not slow down despite draw/qualification incentives.
- `竞彩影响`: 防平、让平/让负/让胜、总进球、比分簇.
