# Postmortem Methodology

Use this reference for post-match review, notable misses, or any fresh analysis where a strong favorite, group-table incentive, draw protection, or overheated Jingcai price may matter.

## Core Lesson

An odds read alone is not enough for Jingcai. A playable view must survive four independent checks:

1. Football context: standings, points, qualification pressure, team ranking/strength gap, form, injuries, lineup, tactical matchup, schedule, and whether one point is acceptable.
2. Jingcai price movement: opening/current HAD and HHAD odds from web sources, with current sellable pools verified.
3. Cross-market comparison: 1X2, let-ball, totals, and score clusters must each be evaluated as possible Jingcai expressions, not only as support or warning.
4. Ticket translation: the final analytical expression must match the best-supported claim, not automatically retreat to 胜平负 or chase the most exciting story.
5. Scorecard discipline: judge the exact `首选` first. A broad direction that was partly right does not rescue a wrong market expression.
6. Calibration discipline: when probability or model language was used, review whether it improved the stance or created false confidence.
7. Variance discipline: do not rewrite the method from one or two outcomes; classify the miss and wait for a meaningful sample before changing thresholds.

## Belgium 0-0 Iran Pattern

The Belgium-Iran miss showed this recurring trap:

- Ranking and reputation favored the strong side, but table state made a draw tolerable.
- Both teams had not created separation in the group, so "favorite should win" was weaker than "favorite cannot lose".
- Main win odds compressed, but the handicap and low-scoring structure did not cleanly support a favorite margin.
- Draw risk was live because the underdog could accept a low-block game and the favorite's dominance did not guarantee repeated high-quality chances.
- Total-goals and exact-score mapping was too aggressive. If 0-0, 1-0, and 1-1 remain live, do not force 3+ goals or favorite let-win.

Use this as a pattern, not a permanent rule against favorites. The lesson is to downgrade from `主胜胆/让胜/大球` to `主胜方向、防平、让球防平/负、小比分` when context and price structure refuse to confirm a clean win.

## Norway 3-2 Senegal / Jordan 1-2 Algeria Let-Draw Lesson

The 2026-06-22 Jingcai sales-day double `Norway -1 draw` + `Jordan +1 draw` showed the opposite trap from over-protecting favorites: correctly seeing "favorite/strong side likely wins but not cleanly" is not enough if the exact one-goal expression is demoted to observation.

Signals that should have upgraded `让平` before kickoff:

- Norway had the stronger win path, but Senegal had must-score motivation and enough resistance/scoring evidence; the better script was Norway by one, not only Norway fail-to-cover.
- Algeria had the win direction, but Jordan's need to respond and prior scoring/resistance made a one-goal away win live; raw away win was safer but less precise.
- Score/total clusters made 2-1, 3-2, and 1-2 visible enough that the exact-margin result could compete with HAD.
- HHAD prices paid for the precision risk and were not merely "higher odds"; they matched the score path.

Review rule: when postmatch shows `让平` hit after being labelled only high-volatility, record whether the pre-match reasoning already named the one-goal score cluster. If yes, the miss is market-expression conservatism, not bad football analysis. Next time, force `让平` to compete for `首选` in the candidate board instead of hiding it below broad win/fail-to-cover protection.

## Final-Round Over-Caution Lesson

The 2026-06-24 World Cup card showed the opposite miss: over-weighting "draw enough", "already qualified", or "favorite may slow down" can erase valid cover paths.

Signals that should prevent automatic downgrade of the stronger side's margin:

- The opponent must chase after conceding, making second-half transition and late-goal scripts stronger than the pre-match low-tempo story.
- The favorite has top-spot, goal-difference, host-statement, record, or substitute-audition incentives even after qualification is likely.
- The favorite's previous process showed repeatable chance creation or the weak side's previous losses showed late separation, discipline problems, or fading legs.
- The weaker side's attack is not strong enough to make 2-1 more natural than 2-0/3-0 when the favorite keeps pushing.
- Rotation quality is asymmetric: favorite substitutes are high-level, while underdog absences reduce resistance.

Review rule: when postmatch shows a favorite cover after the analysis preferred draw/let-draw protection, classify whether the miss came from `末轮过度保守`, `低估替补强度`, `低估弱队追分暴露`, or `低估强队重复创造`. Add a required final-round reversal check before the next slate.

## Required Review Questions

For every postmortem, answer:

- What was the exact pre-match claim: 胜平负, 让球胜平负, 总进球, 比分, 半全场, or parlay role?
- Did the exact `首选` win, lose, or expose that the correct stance should have been `本场不选`?
- Which evidence supported it, and which evidence was ignored?
- Did standings, points, and qualification incentives make one point acceptable?
- Did the analysis also test the opposite final-round script: top-spot/goal-difference/substitute incentives and opponent chase-state after conceding?
- Did the original analysis spell out win/draw/loss consequences and late-game motivation, or merely say "战意强"?
- Did current web-verified Jingcai odds confirm the claim, or only compress the obvious side?
- Did let-ball odds support the margin, or warn that the favorite might win narrowly or fail to win?
- Did the analysis independently compare 让胜、让平、让负, or did it lazily retreat to 胜平负?
- Did totals/score clusters support the selected 总进球 or 比分 range?
- Was the pick a true hit-rate choice, a price-supported value choice, or a narrative chase?
- If probability, model output, xG, or no-vig estimates were used, did they improve calibration or hide the real match risk?
- If an analyst confidence band was stated, was it clearly above market after error buffer, or was the claimed edge too thin?
- Did we have a specific information advantage over the market, or only a duplicated public/model signal?
- Was the event identity correct across sources: match number, kickoff time, home/away order, and neutral-site status?
- What should change next time: skip, defend draw, use let-loss/let-draw, reduce stake, or avoid parlay?

## Prediction Log

For recurring analysis, maintain a compact scorecard when the user asks for learning or when a miss reveals a reusable rule:

| Date | Match | Source quality | Final stance | Market-implied | Confidence band | Actual result | Exact hit? | Miss type | Next rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Classify miss type as one or more of:

- `身份/数据错误`: wrong match, wrong home/away orientation, stale lineup, missing neutral-site context, or odds source mismatch.
- `基本面误判`: tactical shape, motivation, form, injuries, or player state was misread.
- `赔率误判`: source quality, opening/current movement, draw protection, heat, or price burden was misread.
- `市场表达错误`: football direction was reasonable but the exact Jingcai market/result was wrong.
- `模型误用`: model/no-vig/xG signal was treated as stronger than its assumptions allowed.
- `校准过度自信`: confidence band or probability was too high for the evidence.
- `边际过薄`: claimed edge was smaller than source/model/lineup uncertainty.
- `市场效率误判`: no specific information advantage existed against a liquid or well-covered market.
- `应本场不选`: conflict or uncertainty was visible, but the analysis forced a stance.

Use calibration notes only to improve future thresholds. Do not turn postmortem into stake sizing or bankroll advice.

## Calibration and Sample Size

When enough settled stances exist, review calibration by probability/confidence band:

- Fewer than 10 settled stances: only note obvious process errors.
- 10-29 settled stances: classify repeated miss types, but do not draw hit-rate conclusions.
- 30-99 settled stances: compare confidence bands with actual hit rates and market-expression errors.
- 100+ settled stances: adjust thresholds for `价值`, `胆`, `让平`, high-volatility observations, and `本场不选`.

For Brier-style review, score only predictions that had a pre-match numeric probability. Do not invent a probability after the result.

## Fresh Analysis Carryover

Before recommending a fresh Jingcai pick, apply these postmortem lessons:

- Never skip table pressure. State current points, ranking/standing, remaining-match incentives, and whether a draw is strategically useful.
- Never skip event identity. Match number, home/away order, kickoff time, and neutral-site status must match before comparing odds, xG, or model outputs.
- Treat a strong team with compressed win odds as a possible heat trap unless let-ball and score distribution also confirm margin.
- Treat a thin claimed probability edge as no edge unless there is a named information advantage and the gap survives the error buffer.
- When `主胜` probability is high but `让胜` is weak, prefer `主胜方向` or `让球防平/负`; do not call it a clean 胆.
- When draw price is stubbornly low, shortening, or not pushed far enough despite favorite heat, explicitly test 防平.
- When totals are low or compressed, require evidence that the underdog can open the game before recommending 3+ goals.
- When the football read is "strong side wins, weak side scores/resists", do not automatically choose broad win or broad fail-to-cover. Test exact one-goal `让平` as a primary candidate, especially when score/TTG prices keep 2-1, 3-2, 1-2, or 0-1 in the live cluster.
- When the narrative says "final round, favorite may slow down", require a reversal check before downgrading cover: top spot, goal difference, home pressure, substitute quality, weak-side chase state, and whether the favorite can keep creating after 1-0.
- If the only reason for a pick is team fame, ranking gap, or public story, downgrade to observe.
- Make the final recommendation state the failure mode: "最大风险是 0-0/1-1", "最大风险是赢球不穿", or "最大风险是赔率过热".

## Output Requirement

Postmortems should end with reusable learning, not only result accounting:

- `赛前应该看到的信号`
- `实际发生后验证/推翻了什么`
- `首选是否命中，以及是否选错了市场表达`
- `概率/模型校验是否帮忙或误导`
- `校准/样本量结论`
- `以后遇到同类比赛的处理`
- `要写入下次赛前清单的规则`
